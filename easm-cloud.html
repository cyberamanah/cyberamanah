External Attack Surface Management (EASM) & Cloud Security

Page 6 of 10

Domain & DNS Security

  • • Enforce DMARC p=reject or p=quarantine.
  • • Verify SPF record has under 10 DNS lookups and ends in -all.
  • • Audit dangling CNAME records to block subdomain takeovers.

Cloud Storage & Shadow AI

  • • Account-level Block Public Access on AWS S3 & Azure Blobs.
  • • Rotate IAM keys every 90 days or adopt OpenID Connect.
  • • Enforce DLP rules blocking corporate data entry into public AI tools.
